# Quotes plugin

Collect quotations from Draft and Research documents, organize citations with author, year, and page metadata, and drag them directly into your draft.

## Features

- **Context Menu Integration**: Right-click any selected text in Draft or Research (PDF, Word, TXT, Markdown) and choose **Add to quote**.
- **Metadata Management**: Edit author, publication year, page numbers, and source titles for every saved quotation.
- **Drag & Drop Insertion**: Drag quotes onto the editor canvas just like snippets.
- **Automatic References**: Automatically creates or updates `Draft/References.md` with numbered scholarly bibliographic entries.
