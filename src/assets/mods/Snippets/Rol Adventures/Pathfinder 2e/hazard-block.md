### Scythe Blade Pillar — Hazard 2

*Mechanical · Trap*  
**Stealth** DC 18 (trained)  
**Description** Pivoting bronze blades concealed inside a load-bearing stone column sweep the corridor when a pressure plate sinks.

- **Disable:** Thievery DC 18 (trained) to jam the pressure plate, or Athletics DC 20 to wedge the axle.
- **AC** 18; **Fort** +11, **Ref** +5 · **Hardness** 8; **HP** 32 (BT 16)
- **`[reaction]` Whirling Blades (Trigger: A creature steps on the floor tile):** Melee Strike +11 against all creatures in a 10-foot square (`2d8 + 5` slashing damage; critical hit doubles damage and applies `1d4` persistent bleed).
