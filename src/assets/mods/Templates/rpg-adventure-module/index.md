# RPG Adventure - Complete Module

A complete tabletop roleplaying adventure module organized into GM preparation, three playable chapters (Catalyst, Dungeon/Hostile Zone, and Final Confrontation), and two reference appendices for statblocks, items, tables, and player handouts.

## Contents

- **Cover and Synopsis**
- **Game Master Guide and Hooks**
- **Chapter 1 - The Catalyst**
  - 01 - Opening Scene and Encounter
  - 02 - Investigation and Rumors
- **Chapter 2 - The Dungeon or Hostile Zone**
  - 01 - General Overview and Environment
  - 02 - Map and Room Keys
  - 03 - Traps and Hazards
- **Chapter 3 - Final Confrontation**
  - 01 - The Decisive Encounter
  - 02 - Epilogues and Rewards
- **Appendix A - Sheets and Statblocks**
  - Monsters and NPCs
  - Magic Items and Treasure
- **Appendix B - Table Resources**
  - Encounter and Loot Tables
  - Player Handouts and Clues
