# RPG Adventure - Complete Module

This folder is a multi-document template package for tabletop RPG adventure modules.

## Included structure

- `index.md` — Module overview and structural table of contents.
- `chapters/Cover and Synopsis.md` — Module cover, level range, and adventure summary.
- `chapters/Game Master Guide and Hooks.md` — Background lore, pacing notes, and character hooks.
- `chapters/Chapter 1 - The Catalyst/01 - Opening Scene and Encounter.md` — Read-aloud arrival and initial skirmish.
- `chapters/Chapter 1 - The Catalyst/02 - Investigation and Rumors.md` — NPC leads and rumor table.
- `chapters/Chapter 2 - The Dungeon or Hostile Zone/01 - General Overview and Environment.md` — Dungeon features, lighting, and wandering threats.
- `chapters/Chapter 2 - The Dungeon or Hostile Zone/02 - Map and Room Keys.md` — Keyed locations and tactical features.
- `chapters/Chapter 2 - The Dungeon or Hostile Zone/03 - Traps and Hazards.md` — Mechanical and arcane hazards with DCs.
- `chapters/Chapter 3 - Final Confrontation/01 - The Decisive Encounter.md` — Boss arena, phases, and lair actions.
- `chapters/Chapter 3 - Final Confrontation/02 - Epilogues and Rewards.md` — Campaign consequences and victory milestones.
- `chapters/Appendix A - Sheets and Statblocks/Monsters and NPCs.md` — Ready-to-run creature and NPC statblocks.
- `chapters/Appendix A - Sheets and Statblocks/Magic Items and Treasure.md` — Relics, curiosities, and hoard awards.
- `chapters/Appendix B - Table Resources/Encounter and Loot Tables.md` — Rollable random tables.
- `chapters/Appendix B - Table Resources/Player Handouts and Clues.md` — In-world letters, inscriptions, and props.

## Create your own

Copy this folder under `Mods/Templates/<your-id>/`, edit `templateName` in `index.md`, and customize the chapters and appendices inside `chapters/`.
