# Magic Items and Treasure

## Lantern of the Ebb Tide

*Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement)*

While lit, this hooded bronze lantern burns with a cold sea-green flame that remains lit underwater. As an action, you can raise the shutter to reveal hidden runes, invisible ink, or spectral footprints within a 15-foot cone for 1 minute. Once used in this way, the property cannot be used again until the next dawn.
