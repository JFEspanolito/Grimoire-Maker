# Bells of the Sunken Reliquary

*An Adventure Module for 4–5 Characters of Levels 3–5*

:::read-aloud
For three generations the bronze bell atop Crow's Perch Beacon has hung silent, its clapper lost to the sea. Tonight, through a salt fog thick enough to choke a torch, the bell has rung three times.
:::

## Adventure Synopsis

1. **The Catalyst**: The party arrives at the coastal hamlet of Oakhaven as spectral lantern-bearers emerge from the ebb tide.
2. **The Hostile Zone**: Tracing the drowned causeway at low water, the heroes descend into the Sunken Reliquary.
3. **Final Confrontation**: In the flooded nave, the party confronts the Tide-Bound Prior before the equinox surge seals the vault forever.
