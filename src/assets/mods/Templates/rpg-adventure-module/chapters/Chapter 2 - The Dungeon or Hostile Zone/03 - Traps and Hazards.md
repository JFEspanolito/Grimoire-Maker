# Traps and Hazards

## Surge Sluice Trap (R2 Corridor)

- **Trigger**: Stepping on the unmortared flagstone in the center of the passage without wedging the counterweights.
- **Effect**: A pressurized jet of seawater sweeps creatures 20 feet back toward the cistern (**DC 14 Strength saving throw** or take `2d8` bludgeoning damage and fall prone).
- **Countermeasure**: A **DC 13 Dexterity (Thieves' Tools)** check pins the bronze chain inside the wall niche.
