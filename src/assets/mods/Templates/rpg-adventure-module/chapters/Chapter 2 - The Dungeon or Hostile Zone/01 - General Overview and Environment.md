# General Overview and Environment

## Dungeon Features

- **Ceilings & Masonry**: Ribbed basalt arches 15 feet high, slick with phosphorescent kelp.
- **Illumination**: Dim bioluminescent moss in the outer galleries; pitch darkness inside the inner vaults.
- **The Rising Tide**: Every 30 minutes of real exploration time, water levels in the lower chambers rise by 1 foot.
