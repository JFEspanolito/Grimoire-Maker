# Game Master Guide and Hooks

## Adventure Background

A century ago, the wardens of Oakhaven lowered their bronze reliquary into the sea caves beneath the headland to hide its astral astrolabe from corsairs. The wardens' oath bound them to guard the vault until relief arrived—an order that never came.

## Adventure Hooks

| d4 | Hook | Starting Clue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **The Harbor Master's Bounty** | Promises 150 gp to relight the beacon before the merchant convoy arrives. |
| 2 | **A Missing Scholar** | A colleague vanished three nights ago carrying rubbings of the reliquary seal. |
| 3 | **Inherited Key** | A PC carries a tarnished bronze key stamped with the crest of Crow's Perch. |
| 4 | **Omen in the Tide** | Clerics and warlocks hear the submerged bell tolling in their dreams. |
