# Epilogues and Rewards

## Concluding the Adventure

- **The Oath Fulfilled**: Returning the reliquary astrolabe to the beacon tower parts the unnatural fog and earns the Harbor Master's 150 gp reward plus free passage aboard any coastal vessel.
- **The Sealed Vault**: If the party reseals the sea gate before high water, Oakhaven is spared further incursions, and the star chart inside the astrolabe points toward their next adventure.
