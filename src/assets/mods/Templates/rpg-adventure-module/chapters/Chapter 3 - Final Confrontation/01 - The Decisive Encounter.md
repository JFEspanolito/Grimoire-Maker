# The Decisive Encounter

:::read-aloud
At the far end of the flooded nave, a figure in salt-crusted chainmail rises from behind the bronze reliquary. As he lifts his lantern, the water around the pillars begins to spin into a slow whirlpool.
:::

## Encounter Phases

1. **Phase 1 — The Oath Test**: The **Tide-Bound Prior** demands the relief watchword. If the party presents the bronze seal and speaks the keepers' names, he hesitates for 1 round.
2. **Phase 2 — Undertow Surge**: At half hit points, the Prior rings the sanctuary bell, pulling all creatures within 20 feet 10 feet closer on initiative count 20.
