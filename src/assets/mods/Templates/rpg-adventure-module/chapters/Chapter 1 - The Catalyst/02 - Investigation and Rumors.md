# Investigation and Rumors

## Key Locales in Oakhaven

- **The Salt-Rimed Anchor**: Tavern kept by **Mara Venn**, who remembers the old rhyme that opens the sea gate.
- **Parish Vestry**: Holds the water-stained register listing the names of the last seven beacon keepers.

## Rumor Table

| d6 | Rumor | Truth |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–2 | "The causeway only surfaces for two hours before the new moon." | **True** — lingering longer risks being trapped by the tide. |
| 3–4 | "Smugglers use the beacon cellar to store brandy." | **False** — the smugglers fled weeks ago after hearing chains below. |
| 5–6 | "Speaking a warden's name aloud pacifies the guardians." | **Partially True** — grants advantage on Charisma checks with the Prior. |
