# Opening Scene and Encounter

:::read-aloud
Rain needles the black shingles of Oakhaven's quay. Below the timber pier, pale green lantern light rises through six feet of churning seawater, and barnacle-crusted boots find purchase on the lower rungs of the pilot's ladder.
:::

## Tactical Setup

- **Terrain**: Slick timber pilings require a **DC 11 Dexterity (Acrobatics)** check when dashing.
- **Foes**: Three **Drowned Watchmen** climb onto the pier to reclaim the harbor chain.
- **Development**: Defeating or turning the watchmen reveals a bronze seal token wedged inside the leader's gauntlet.
