# In Praise of Visible Seams

**M**odern interfaces conspire to hide how things work. Corners are smoothed, screws are sealed beneath adhesive, and files vanish into nameless clouds. Yet the craftsperson—whether bookbinder, lutemaker, or typesetter—knows that an honest joint is stronger than a concealed one.

When you can see the thread in the spine of a booklet, you know how to repair it when it wears thin.
