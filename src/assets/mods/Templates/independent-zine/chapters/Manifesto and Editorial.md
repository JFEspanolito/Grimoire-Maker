# Manifesto and Editorial

We print on paper because paper remembers the pressure of the hand. Every issue of *Signal & Static* is assembled as a self-contained artifact—meant to be folded, passed across a studio table, and annotated in the margins.

In this seventh issue, we examine why small, durable tools outlast the platforms built to replace them.
