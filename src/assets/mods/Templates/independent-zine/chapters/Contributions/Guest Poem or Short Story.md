# The Last Typesetter of Dock Street

*Guest contribution by M. K. Solis*

Every evening at six, Arthur locked the street door, switched on the amber lamp above the California job case, and composed a single paragraph describing the weather over the harbor. He pulled one proof on dampened rag paper, hung it to dry from a clothespress wire, and distributed the lead type back into its compartments before dawn.
