# Studio Conversation: Building for Longevity

**S&S:** Why return to plain-text folders and physical booklets when everything can live in a browser tab?

**Sora Lin:** Because a folder of plain text files on your own drive will still open twenty years from now. You own the structure, the archive, and the rhythm of your work.
