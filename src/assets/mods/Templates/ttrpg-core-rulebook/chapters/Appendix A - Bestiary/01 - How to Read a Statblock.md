# Anatomy of a Creature Statblock

Every adversary entry in this bestiary includes:

- **Name, Tier & Role:** Indicates the creature's combat function (Minion, Skirmisher, Bruiser, Artillery, Leader, or Solo).
- **Defenses & Vitality:** Armor Class / Defense, Hit Points, Resistances, and Saving Throw modifiers.
- **Signature Actions & Reactions:** Ready-to-run attacks, recharge abilities, and tactical triggers.
