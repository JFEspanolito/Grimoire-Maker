# Action Resolution & Checks

## Difficulty Scale

| Difficulty Tier | Target Number (DC) | Benchmark Example |
| --- | :---: | --- |
| **Routine** | 10 | Climb a knotted rope, recall common local lore |
| **Challenging** | 14 | Pick a merchant's lock, navigate a fog bank |
| **Formidable** | 18 | Disarm an arcane ward, swim against a flash flood |
| **Heroic** | 22 | Leap a chasm in full armor, decipher an elder seal |

## Degrees of Success

- **Critical Success (DC + 10 or Natural Max):** Achieve the objective with an added tactical benefit.
- **Success (Meets DC):** Achieve the stated goal cleanly.
- **Failure (Below DC):** Fall short or succeed at a cost / complication.
