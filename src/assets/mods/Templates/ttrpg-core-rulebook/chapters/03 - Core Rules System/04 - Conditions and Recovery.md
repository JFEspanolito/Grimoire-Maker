# Conditions, Injuries & Recovery

## Common Conditions

- **Dazed:** Can take either a Move or Main Action on a turn, not both; cannot take Reactions.
- **Exposed / Off-Guard:** Suffers -2 Defense against incoming attacks.
- **Hindered:** Movement speed is halved; cannot Dash.

## Resting & Recovery

- **Breather (10 minutes):** Spend recovery dice to restore lost Hit Points.
- **Full Rest (8 hours in safety):** Restore all Hit Points, half of spent recovery dice, and remove 1 level of Fatigue.
