# Combat & Tactics

## Turn Structure & Action Economy

On your turn in the Initiative order, you can perform:
1. **Main Action:** Attack, Cast a Power, Dash, Disengage, or Use an Object.
2. **Move Action:** Move up to your Speed across unobstructed terrain.
3. **Reaction (1/Round):** Triggered off-turn by a specific condition (e.g., Opportunity Strike or Shield Block).

## Attack & Damage Resolution

Roll `Attack Die + Attribute + Proficiency` against the target's Defense. On a hit, roll weapon damage and subtract the target's Armor Reduction (if applicable).
