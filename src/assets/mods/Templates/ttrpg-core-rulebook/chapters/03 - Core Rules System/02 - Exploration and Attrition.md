# Exploration & Attrition

## Exploration Turns & Travel Watches

Dungeon exploration is tracked in 10-minute Exploration Turns; overland travel is divided into four 6-hour Watches per day.

## Environmental Hazards & Supply Depletion

- **Torches & Light:** A standard torch burns for 6 Exploration Turns (1 hour).
- **Rations & Thirst:** Each character consumes 1 ration and 1 waterskin per day; going without inflicts 1 level of Fatigue.
