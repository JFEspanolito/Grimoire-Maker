# Welcome & Core Concept

## What Is This Game?

Introduce the setting, the role of the players, and the three core pillars of play (Exploration, Tactical Conflict, and Consequence).

## Tone & Themes

- **High Stakes Exploration:** Every journey beyond the frontier requires preparation and resource management.
- **Tactical Teamwork:** Victory relies on positioning, synergy, and exploiting environmental advantages.
