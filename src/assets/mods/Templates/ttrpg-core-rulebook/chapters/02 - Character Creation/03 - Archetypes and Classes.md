# Archetypes & Classes

## The Vanguard

*Front-line defenders who anchor the formation and shield allies from overwhelming force.*

- **Primary Attributes:** Might & Willpower
- **Starting Vitality:** `12 + Constitution`
- **Core Feature — Bulwark Stance:** While wielding a shield or heavy weapon, adjacent allies gain +1 Defense against ranged and area attacks.
