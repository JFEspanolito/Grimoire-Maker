# Step-by-Step Character Creation

1. **Choose Concept & Origin:** Define where your character comes from and their place in the world.
2. **Select an Archetype / Class:** Determine your combat role, core proficiencies, and signature features.
3. **Assign Attributes:** Distribute the standard array or point-buy budget across your core attributes.
4. **Pick Skills & Talents:** Customize your expertise and background knacks.
5. **Equip & Calculate Derived Stats:** Record Hit Points, Defense, Initiative, and starting kit.
