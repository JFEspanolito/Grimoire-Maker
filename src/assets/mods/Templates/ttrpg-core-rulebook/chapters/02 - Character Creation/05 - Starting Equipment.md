# Starting Equipment & Encumbrance

## Starting Packs

- **Expedition Pack (25 gp):** Backpack, bedroll, 50 ft. hempen rope, grappling hook, 5 torches, flint and steel, 7 days of rations.
- **Scholar's Pack (30 gp):** Leather satchel, blank vellum folio, ink and brass nibs, magnifying lens, sealing wax.
- **Encumbrance Rule:** A character can carry a number of Bulk slots equal to `5 + Might` without penalty.
