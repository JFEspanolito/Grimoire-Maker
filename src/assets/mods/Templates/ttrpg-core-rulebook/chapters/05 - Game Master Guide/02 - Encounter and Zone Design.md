# Building Encounters, Zones & Hazards

## Encounter Budget by Party Size

| Encounter Threat | Budget per Hero | Recommended Composition |
| --- | :---: | --- |
| **Skirmish (Low)** | 1 Threat Point | 2–3 Minions or 1 Standard foe |
| **Standard (Moderate)** | 2 Threat Points | 1 Leader + 2 Standard foes + 1 terrain feature |
| **Climactic (Boss)** | 3 Threat Points | 1 Solo/Boss + dynamic hazard or lair phase |
