# Progression, Rewards & Downtime

## Milestone & Experience Advancement

Characters advance when they complete major quests, uncover lost sanctuaries, or survive high-stakes expeditions.

## Downtime Activities

Between adventures, heroes can spend 1 Downtime Week to **Recover from Injuries**, **Craft or Upgrade Gear**, **Research Lore**, or **Build Faction Standing**.
