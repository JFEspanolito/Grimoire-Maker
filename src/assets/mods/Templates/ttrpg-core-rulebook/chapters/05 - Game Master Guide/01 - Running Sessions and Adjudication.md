# Running Sessions & Adjudication

## Principles for the Game Master

1. **Telegraph Danger Honestly:** Give players clear sensory cues before springing lethal hazards.
2. **Call for Rolls Only When Outcomes Are Uncertain:** If a character has time, tools, and training without pressure, let them succeed.
3. **Make Every Roll Move the Fiction Forward:** Even on a failed check, change the situation rather than stalling at a closed door.
