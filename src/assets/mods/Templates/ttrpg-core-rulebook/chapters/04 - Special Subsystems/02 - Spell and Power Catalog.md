# Spell & Power Catalog

#### Aegis of Starlight — Tier 1 Ward

- **Cost:** 2 Mana / 1 Action · **Range:** Self or Close Ally · **Duration:** 1 Scene

Wreathes the target in silver light, granting +2 Defense and resistance to necrotic or shadow damage until the ward absorbs 10 points of damage.

#### Shattering Resonance — Tier 2 Evocation

- **Cost:** 4 Mana / 1 Action · **Range:** 60 ft. (15 ft. burst) · **Save:** Fortitude

Unleashes a concussive chime that deals `3d8` thunder damage and pushes targets 10 feet away on a failed save.
