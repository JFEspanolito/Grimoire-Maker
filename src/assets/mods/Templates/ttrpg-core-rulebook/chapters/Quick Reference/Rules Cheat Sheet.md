# Quick Reference — Rules Cheat Sheet

## Difficulty Benchmarks

| Difficulty | DC | Turn Economy |
| --- | :---: | --- |
| **Routine** | 10 | **1 Main Action** (Attack, Cast, Dash, Interact) |
| **Challenging** | 14 | **1 Move Action** (Stride up to Speed) |
| **Formidable** | 18 | **1 Reaction** per round |
| **Heroic** | 22 | **1 Free Object Interaction** |

## Combat Modifiers

- **Cover (Half / Three-Quarters):** +2 / +4 Defense
- **Flanking / High Ground:** +2 to Attack Rolls
