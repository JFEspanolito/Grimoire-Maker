# Rare Artifacts & Unique Relics

## The Meridian Astrolabe

*Unique Relic · Requires Attunement*

Forged from star-brass and nested quartz rings, this astrolabe maps leylines across the realm.

- **Compass of the Unseen:** While carried, the bearer cannot become lost in magical fog or shifting labyrinths.
- **Fold the Horizon (1/Day):** Expend 1 charge to open a brief passage between two known stone arches within 1 mile.
