# III. Dusk

:::box{width="65%" align="center"}
<p style="text-align: center">Amber gathers in the western eaves<br>like resin sealing an ancient letter.<br>The hills fold their maps one ridge at a time.</p>

<p style="text-align: center">We speak more softly when the lamps are lit,<br>as though the dark outside the windowpane<br>were listening for its name.</p>
:::
