# IV. Midnight

:::box{width="65%" align="center"}
<p style="text-align: center">Twelve strokes dissolve into the frost.<br>Only the embers keep their small republic<br>behind the iron grate.</p>

<p style="text-align: center">What could not be said at noon<br>is written now in ash and salt<br>for the morning tide to read.</p>
:::
