# I. Dawn

:::box{width="65%" align="center"}
<p style="text-align: center">Before the bell tower names the hour,<br>the river unfastens its coat of mist<br>and lays gray silver against the stones.</p>

<p style="text-align: center">One reed bends where the heron stood;<br>the water remembers the weight of wings<br>long after the sky has taken them back.</p>
:::
