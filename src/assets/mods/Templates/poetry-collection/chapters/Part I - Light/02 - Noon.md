# II. Noon

:::box{width="65%" align="center"}
<p style="text-align: center">At zenith the courtyard loses its shadows;<br>even the sundial holds its breath<br>beneath the white geometry of summer.</p>

<p style="text-align: center">Figs ripen in the dry stone wall,<br>and the cistern keeps its cool dark coin<br>unspent beneath the glare.</p>
:::
