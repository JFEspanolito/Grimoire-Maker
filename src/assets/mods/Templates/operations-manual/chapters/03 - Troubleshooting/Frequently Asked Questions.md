# Frequently Asked Questions

### Where are my custom templates and snippets stored?

By default, user mods live inside your application configuration directory under `Mods/Templates`, `Mods/Snippets`, and `Mods/Plugins`. You can customize these directories at any time from the Library settings.

### Can I share a manual structure with my team?

Yes. Use **Export Template ZIP** from the Template Catalog to bundle your template folder and share it across workstations.
