# Home Before Sunset

## Complete success

The courier and medicine arrive safely. The town offers shelter to the families.

## Partial success

Some supplies are lost. Decide who helps the town through the winter and what favor they request.

## Rewards

- The promised payment.
- An ally at the apothecary.
- A rumor about an abandoned storehouse beyond the pass.

## Closing question

Ask each player which stranger their character now considers a friend.
