# The Old Bridge

## Arrival

An overturned cart blocks the bridge. A strip of blue cloth hangs from the railing.

## Evidence

| Detail | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Small footprints in wet clay | Several travelers left together |
| An unopened medicine crate | Theft was not the original plan |
| Rope tied beneath the bridge | Someone descended into the ravine |

## Complication

Rising water threatens the route below. The party can secure the rope, find another path, or negotiate with the people hiding there.
