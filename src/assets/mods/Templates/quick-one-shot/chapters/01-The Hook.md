# The Missing Courier

A courier carrying the town's winter medicine disappeared at the old bridge. A storm will close the pass at sunset.

## Briefing

The apothecary offers payment for the medicine, with a bonus for bringing the courier home.

## Three questions

- Who last saw the courier?
- What tracks lead away from the bridge?
- Why was the medicine worth stealing?

## Pacing

Spend roughly one quarter of the session here. Give the party a clear trail even if their investigation fails.
