# Shelter in the Ravine

The courier is sheltering with displaced families. Their youngest child needs the same medicine as the town.

## Stakes

There is not enough medicine for everyone unless the party retrieves a second crate from the flooded cart.

## Approaches

- Organize a rescue before the storm peaks.
- Negotiate how supplies will be shared.
- Confront a scavenger who has taken the remaining crate.

Choose a challenge suited to your rules system. Keep the rescue objective visible throughout the encounter.
