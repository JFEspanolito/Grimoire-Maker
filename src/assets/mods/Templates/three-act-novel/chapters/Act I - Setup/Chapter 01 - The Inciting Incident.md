# Chapter 01 — The Inciting Incident

**T**he brass pendulum inside the observatory had not swung since the winter of the great flood, yet at three minutes past midnight Clara heard its gear teeth catch against the escapement.

<p style="text-align: center">⁂</p>

She set her caliper down beside the ledger and held the lantern toward the vaulted ceiling. Where twelve Roman numerals had been engraved into the limestone ring, a narrow seam of gilt bronze now gleamed between XII and I.
