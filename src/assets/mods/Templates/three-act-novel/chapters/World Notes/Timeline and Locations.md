# Timeline and Locations

## Key Chronology

- **Year 0**: Founding of the Meridian Charter and construction of the Royal Observatory.
- **Year 84**: The Great Flood submerges the Outer Wards; the Censorate seals the archives.
- **Year 102 (Present)**: The thirteenth hour chimes; Clara begins her journey.

## Major Locations

- **The Royal Observatory**: Perched above the basalt cliffs overlooking the inner harbor.
- **Greywater Lock**: The fog-bound canal gate separating the capital from the sunken viaducts.
- **The Solstice Tower**: The clockwork regulator anchoring the city's boundaries.
