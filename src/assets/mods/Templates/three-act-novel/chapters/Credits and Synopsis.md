# Credits and Synopsis

## Logline

When an apprentice cartographer discovers a thirteenth hour chiming inside the Royal Observatory, she must trace a vanished expedition before the city's borders erase themselves.

## Three-Act Synopsis

- **Act I — Setup**: Clara repairs a broken astrolabe that points to an uncharted district and is forced to leave the guild archive.
- **Act II — Confrontation**: Pursued by the Censorate, she crosses the Sunken Viaduct and learns the city's founders traded memory for permanence.
- **Act III — Resolution**: At the Solstice Tower, Clara must choose between restoring the lost districts or preserving the fragile peace of the capital.
