# Novel - Three Acts

This folder is a multi-document template package for long-form fiction organized around the classical three-act structure.

## Included structure

- `index.md` — Manuscript overview and structural map.
- `chapters/Cover.md` — Title page and edition imprint.
- `chapters/Credits and Synopsis.md` — Copyright notice, pitch, and three-act synopsis.
- `chapters/Act I - Setup/Chapter 01 - The Inciting Incident.md` — Ordinary world and catalyst.
- `chapters/Act I - Setup/Chapter 02 - The Departure.md` — Crossing the first threshold.
- `chapters/Act II - Confrontation/Chapter 03 - First Obstacle.md` — Rising trials and alliances.
- `chapters/Act II - Confrontation/Chapter 04 - Point of No Return.md` — Midpoint shift and escalating stakes.
- `chapters/Act III - Resolution/Chapter 05 - The Climax.md` — Final confrontation.
- `chapters/Act III - Resolution/Chapter 06 - Resolution.md` — Aftermath and closing image.
- `chapters/World Notes/Character Profiles.md` — Protagonist, antagonist, and supporting cast reference.
- `chapters/World Notes/Timeline and Locations.md` — Chronology and setting bible.

## Create your own

Copy this folder under `Mods/Templates/<your-id>/`, update `templateName` in `index.md`, and add or rearrange chapters inside `chapters/`.
