# The Night Market

At midnight, the station's unused platform fills with stalls. Every seller accepts a different currency.

## Three stalls

- **The Mapmaker:** Trades impossible routes for stories of home.
- **The Candle Seller:** Exchanges one hour of sleep for a light that reveals secrets.
- **The Lost Property Desk:** Returns an object you have not lost yet.

## A question to explore

What would you trade for a safe journey to somewhere you can never visit again?
