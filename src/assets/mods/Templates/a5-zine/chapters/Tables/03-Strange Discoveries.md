# Strange Discoveries

Roll a die or choose a detail.

| d6 | Discovery |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | A ticket stamped with tomorrow's date |
| 2 | A cup that stays warm until its owner tells the truth |
| 3 | A suitcase full of unfamiliar family photographs |
| 4 | A bell that rings only when nobody is listening |
| 5 | A timetable with one destination crossed out |
| 6 | A letter addressed to the reader |

Combine two results to create a scene, then write what changed when morning arrived.
