# Family Cookbook

This folder is a multi-document template package for recipe collections, culinary guides, and family cookbooks.

## Included structure

- `index.md` — Cookbook overview and course directory.
- `chapters/Cover.md` — Title page and family kitchen dedication.
- `chapters/Introduction and Kitchen Tips.md` — Pantry staples, weights, and temperature conversions.
- `chapters/Starters and Appetizers/Recipe 01 - Seasonal Salad.md` — Fresh starter template.
- `chapters/Starters and Appetizers/Recipe 02 - Rustic Soup.md` — Broth and soup template.
- `chapters/Main Courses/Recipe 03 - Artisan Pasta.md` — Handmade pasta template.
- `chapters/Main Courses/Recipe 04 - Traditional Stew.md` — Slow-braised dish template.
- `chapters/Desserts and Drinks/Recipe 05 - Homemade Tart.md` — Pastry and baking template.
- `chapters/Desserts and Drinks/Recipe 06 - Infusions and Cocktails.md` — Beverage and botanical infusion template.
- `chapters/Index by Main Ingredient.md` — Cross-reference table organized by primary ingredient.

## Create your own

Copy this folder under `Mods/Templates/<your-id>/`, edit `templateName` in `index.md`, and add your own courses and recipes under `chapters/`.
