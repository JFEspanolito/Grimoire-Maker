# Recipe 03 — Hand-Cut Tagliatelle with Brown Butter & Sage

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Servings | Difficulty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 40 min | 10 min | 4 | Medium |

## Ingredients

- 300 g tipo 00 flour and 3 whole farm eggs
- 85 g unsalted cultured butter
- 16 fresh sage leaves
- 60 g aged Parmigiano-Reggiano, finely grated

## Preparation

1. **Make the dough**: Knead flour and eggs for 10 minutes until smooth and elastic; rest wrapped for 30 minutes before rolling and cutting into 7 mm ribbons.
2. **Infuse the butter**: Melt butter in a wide skillet until nutty and golden, crisping the sage leaves for the final 60 seconds.
3. **Emulsify**: Boil pasta in salted water for 2 minutes, transfer directly into the skillet with a splash of pasta water, and toss with Parmigiano.
