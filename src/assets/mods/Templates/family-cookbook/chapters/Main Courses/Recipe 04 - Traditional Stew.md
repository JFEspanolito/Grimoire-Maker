# Recipe 04 — Slow-Braised Orchard Beef & Root Vegetables

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Servings | Difficulty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 25 min | 2 hr 30 min | 6 | Medium |

## Ingredients

- 1 kg chuck roast, cut into 4 cm cubes
- 300 ml dry apple cider and 500 ml beef stock
- 4 parsnips and 4 carrots, cut on the bias
- 2 tbsp tomato paste and 3 sprigs fresh thyme

## Preparation

1. **Sear**: Brown the seasoned beef in batches in a heavy Dutch oven and set aside.
2. **Deglaze**: Caramelize tomato paste in the fond, pour in the dry cider, and scrape the pot clean.
3. **Braise**: Return beef with stock and thyme; cover and braise at 160 °C for 2 hours, adding root vegetables for the final 45 minutes.
