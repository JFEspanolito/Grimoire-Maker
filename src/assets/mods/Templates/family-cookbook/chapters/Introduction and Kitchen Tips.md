# Introduction and Kitchen Tips

## Pantry Essentials

- Cold-pressed olive oil, flaky sea salt, and whole black peppercorns.
- Homemade vegetable or poultry stock frozen in 500 ml portions.
- Fresh hardy herbs: rosemary, thyme, and bay leaf.

## Measurement Reference

| Volume | Metric Equivalent | Common Ingredient |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 tablespoon | 15 ml | Olive oil, vinegar, honey |
| 1 cup | 240 ml | Broth, milk, water |
| 1 cup (flour) | 125 g | All-purpose wheat flour |
