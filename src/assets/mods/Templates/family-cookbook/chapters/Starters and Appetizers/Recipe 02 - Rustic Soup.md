# Recipe 02 — Tuscan White Bean & Rosemary Soup

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Servings | Difficulty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 15 min | 35 min | 4 | Easy |

## Ingredients

- 400 g cooked cannellini beans, rinsed
- 1 yellow onion, 2 carrots, and 2 celery stalks, finely diced
- 3 cloves garlic, smashed
- 1.2 L rich vegetable stock and 1 sprig fresh rosemary
- 4 slices toasted sourdough rubbed with garlic

## Preparation

1. **Sweat the aromatics**: Cook onion, carrot, and celery in olive oil over medium-low heat for 10 minutes until translucent.
2. **Simmer**: Add garlic, rosemary, cannellini beans, and stock; simmer gently for 25 minutes.
3. **Finish**: Blend one third of the soup for body, return to the pot, and ladle over toasted sourdough.
