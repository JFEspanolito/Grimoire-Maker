# Recipe 01 — Roasted Fig & Bitter Greens Salad

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Servings | Difficulty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 15 min | 10 min | 4 | Easy |

## Ingredients

- 200 g radicchio and arugula, washed and spun dry
- 6 ripe mission figs, halved lengthwise
- 80 g aged goat cheese, crumbled
- 40 g toasted walnuts, coarsely chopped
- 2 tbsp aged balsamic reduction and 3 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil

## Preparation

1. **Roast the figs**: Arrange halved figs cut-side up on a parchment tray and broil for 6–8 minutes until caramelized.
2. **Dress the greens**: Toss radicchio and arugula with olive oil, flaky salt, and cracked pepper just before serving.
3. **Plate**: Layer warm figs, crumbled goat cheese, and toasted walnuts over the greens; finish with balsamic reduction.
