# Recipe 05 — Spiced Pear & Almond Frangipane Tart

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Servings | Difficulty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 30 min | 45 min | 8 | Medium |

## Ingredients

- 1 blind-baked 24 cm sweet shortcrust pastry shell
- 120 g almond flour, 100 g softened butter, and 100 g caster sugar
- 2 eggs and 1 tsp vanilla bean paste
- 3 firm Bosc pears, peeled, cored, and fanned

## Preparation

1. **Prepare the frangipane**: Cream butter and sugar until pale, beat in eggs one at a time, and fold in almond flour and vanilla.
2. **Assemble**: Spread frangipane evenly inside the cooled tart shell and arrange fanned pear halves radially on top.
3. **Bake**: Bake at 180 °C for 40–45 minutes until deeply golden and set in the center.
