# Recipe 06 — Smoked Citrus & Verbena Botanical Infusion

| Prep Time | Cook Time | Servings | Difficulty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10 min | 10 min | 2 | Easy |

## Ingredients

- 4 strips charred blood orange peel
- 1 handful dried lemon verbena leaves
- 1 tbsp wildflower honey
- 500 ml freshly boiled spring water (or chilled sparkling water for a cold spritz)

## Preparation

1. **Steep**: Combine charred orange peel and lemon verbena in a warmed ceramic pot; pour over hot water and cover for 6 minutes.
2. **Sweeten & Serve**: Stir in wildflower honey and strain into warmed glasses, or chill over a large ice sphere and top with soda.
