# Thesis Example

This folder provides a multi-document template package for academic theses, dissertations, and formal scholarly monographs.

## Included structure

- `index.md` — Thesis overview and table of contents.
- `chapters/00 - Front Matter/Title and Authorship.md` — Title page, candidate credentials, advisor, faculty, institution, and defense date.
- `chapters/00 - Front Matter/Approval Sheet.md` — Evaluation committee approval record and signature certification.
- `chapters/00 - Front Matter/Dedication and Acknowledgements.md` — Personal dedications and academic support recognitions.
- `chapters/00 - Front Matter/Abstract and Keywords.md` — Structured abstract summarizing context, methods, core findings, and keywords.
- `chapters/01 - Introduction/Problem Statement.md` — Problem formulation, background context, and core research questions.
- `chapters/01 - Introduction/Objectives and Justification.md` — General and specific objectives, theoretical relevance, and delimitations.
- `chapters/02 - Literature Review/Theoretical Framework.md` — Theoretical foundations, conceptual models, and construct definitions.
- `chapters/02 - Literature Review/State of the Art.md` — Critical appraisal of empirical literature, research gap, and hypotheses.
- `chapters/03 - Methodology/Research Design and Approach.md` — Epistemological framework, research typology, and procedural design.
- `chapters/03 - Methodology/Population and Sample.md` — Target population, sampling strategy, sample size, and criteria.
- `chapters/03 - Methodology/Instruments and Data Collection.md` — Data gathering protocols, measurement scales, validity, and reliability.
- `chapters/03 - Methodology/Data Analysis and Ethical Standards.md` — Statistical/qualitative analytical techniques and institutional ethics clearance.
- `chapters/04 - Results/Data Presentation and Findings.md` — Empirical observations, descriptive statistics, tables, and figures.
- `chapters/04 - Results/Hypothesis Testing.md` — Inferential statistical tests, effect sizes, and model verification.
- `chapters/05 - Discussion/Interpretation and Theoretical Implications.md` — Analytical interpretation contrasting findings against literature.
- `chapters/05 - Discussion/Limitations and Future Research.md` — Methodological constraints, boundary conditions, and future lines of inquiry.
- `chapters/06 - Conclusions and Recommendations/Conclusions.md` — Synthesis of results addressing the stated research objectives.
- `chapters/06 - Conclusions and Recommendations/Recommendations.md` — Actionable proposals for academic research, practitioners, and policy.
- `chapters/Appendices/References.md` — Complete reference list formatted according to citation standards (APA/IEEE/Chicago).
- `chapters/Appendices/Data Collection Instruments.md` — Questionnaires, interview guides, and observation protocols.
- `chapters/Appendices/Supplemental Data and Approvals.md` — Extended datasets, proof transcripts, and IRB/ethics approvals.

## Create your own

Copy this folder under `Mods/Templates/<your-id>/`, edit `templateName` in `index.md`, and author each section inside `chapters/`.
