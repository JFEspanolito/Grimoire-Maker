# State of the Art

The state of the art provides a systematic, critical appraisal of recent empirical studies published within the last three to five years, uncovering the specific research gap your thesis addresses.

## Guidelines for this Section

- **Empirical Precedents**: Review high-impact peer-reviewed journal articles, conference proceedings, and doctoral dissertations that investigate related research questions.
- **Critical Comparison Matrix**: Summarize key precedents across standardized criteria:
  - *Authors & Year*
  - *Context & Sample*
  - *Methodology & Instruments*
  - *Core Findings*
  - *Methodological Limitations or Gaps*
- **Identification of the Research Gap**: Conclude the section by clearly showing what remains unresolved, conflicting, or unverified in previous studies, justifying the current research.
- **Hypotheses or Research Propositions**:
  - *General Hypothesis ($H_1$)*: The proposed causal relationship, correlation, or system effect expected from the study.
  - *Null Hypothesis ($H_0$)*: The statement asserting no significant relationship, difference, or effect.
  - *Operational Hypotheses*: Detailed statistical statements for sub-dimensions of the inquiry.
