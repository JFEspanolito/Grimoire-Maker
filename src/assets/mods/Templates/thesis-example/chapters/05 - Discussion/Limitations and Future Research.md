# Limitations and Future Research

Academic rigor requires transparent and self-critical acknowledgment of methodological constraints, paired with constructive guidance for subsequent investigations.

## Study Limitations

Detail the boundaries of the investigation honestly; acknowledging limitations enhances scholarly credibility:

- **Internal Validity Constraints**: Potential confounding factors, unmeasured covariates, instrument ceiling/floor effects, or participant fatigue during experiments.
- **External Validity and Generalizability**: Constraints imposed by sample size, geographic concentration, specific hardware/software configurations, or organizational culture that limit transferring conclusions to disparate environments.
- **Measurement and Technical Limitations**: Latency precision limits, reliance on self-reported survey responses, or observational blind spots.

## Future Research Directions

Formulate specific, non-trivial recommendations for follow-up investigations:

- Propose concrete methodological refinements (e.g., longitudinal observation across multi-year cycles).
- Suggest testing the proposed models in alternative domains, larger demographics, or under edge-case operational stress.
- Identify new research questions that emerged unexpectedly from the findings.
