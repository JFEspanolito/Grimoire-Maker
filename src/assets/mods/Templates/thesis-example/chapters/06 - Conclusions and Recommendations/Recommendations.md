# Recommendations

Recommendations translate academic conclusions into actionable, pragmatic proposals targeted at specific stakeholders and institutions.

## Structuring Actionable Recommendations

Categorize recommendations by target stakeholder group:

1. **For the Scientific Community and Academic Institutions**:
   - Specific methodological standardizations for subsequent researchers replicating this study.
   - Theoretical extensions to be integrated into graduate curricula or specialized research groups.
2. **For Industry Practitioners and Engineers**:
   - Concrete architectural patterns, refactoring roadmaps, or operational best practices derived from empirical findings.
   - Cost-benefit considerations and implementation timelines for adopting the proposed solutions.
3. **For Policy Makers and Regulatory Bodies**:
   - Institutional guidelines, compliance benchmarks, or regulatory adjustments justified by the research evidence.
