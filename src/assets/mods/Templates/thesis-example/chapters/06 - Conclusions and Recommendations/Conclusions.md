# Conclusions

The conclusions synthesize the core contributions of the dissertation, demonstrating how the investigation fulfilled its stated general and specific research objectives.

## Guidelines for Authoring Conclusions

- **Alignment with Objectives**: Write one clear, dedicated conclusion paragraph for each specific research objective formulated in Chapter 1, followed by an overarching conclusion addressing the general objective.
- **Synthesized Evidence, Not Raw Data**: Avoid re-listing exact numerical data, statistical tables, or extensive $p$-values. State what the findings mean at an epistemic and practical level.
- **Strict Grounding in Evidence**: Never introduce new assertions, unverified claims, or speculation not substantiated by the data presented in Chapter 4 and analyzed in Chapter 5.
- **Scholarly Contribution**: End with a closing synthesis summarizing the core legacy and value of the dissertation for the academic community.
