# Supplemental Data and Approvals

This appendix archives auxiliary technical datasets, full statistical output tables, regulatory authorizations, and formal institutional ethics documentation.

## Documentation Checklist

Organize supplementary documentation into clearly indexed exhibits:

- **Appendix A: Institutional Review Board (IRB) Approval Letter**: Official certificate of ethical clearance granted by the institutional oversight committee.
- **Appendix B: Institutional Access Authorizations**: Formal letters granting access to enterprise systems, proprietary codebases, or institutional premises.
- **Appendix C: Participant Informed Consent Forms**: Blank template of the informed consent and assent documents executed by study participants.
- **Appendix D: Supplemental Statistical Tables**: Comprehensive ANOVA summary tables, full correlation matrices, factor loading matrices, and statistical power diagnostic plots.
- **Appendix E: System Configuration and Environment Schematics**: Detailed hardware topology diagrams, dependency manifests, and infrastructure environment variables.
