# Research Design and Approach

This section establishes the epistemological approach and operational design of the study, ensuring methodological reproducibility and scientific validity.

## Dimensions of the Research Design

Detail each formal dimension of your methodological framework:

1. **Epistemological Paradigm**:
   - *Positivist / Quantitative*: Emphasizes objective measurement, causal inference, and statistical generalization.
   - *Interpretivist / Constructivist / Qualitative*: Explores subjective meanings, lived experiences, and contextual interpretations.
   - *Pragmatist / Mixed-Methods*: Combines quantitative breadth with qualitative depth (e.g., convergent parallel or sequential explanatory design).
2. **Research Level / Scope**:
   - *Exploratory*: Investigates under-theorized phenomena to generate novel hypotheses.
   - *Descriptive*: Accurately profiles characteristics, frequencies, and distributions.
   - *Correlational*: Evaluates non-causal statistical associations between variables.
   - *Explanatory / Causal*: Isolates causal mechanisms and tests direct effects.
3. **Temporal Design**:
   - *Cross-Sectional*: Observations collected at a single distinct point in time.
   - *Longitudinal*: Repeated observations across extended durations to trace evolution over time.
4. **Experimental Manipulation**:
   - *Experimental*: Random assignment of subjects into control and experimental groups with active independent variable manipulation.
   - *Quasi-Experimental*: Pre-post interventions conducted without strict random assignment.
   - *Non-Experimental / Ex Post Facto*: Observational study of naturally occurring variables.
