## Psychometric and Measurement Validation

- **Content and Construct Validity**: Report expert judgment evaluation (e.g., Aiken's $V$ or Lawshe's Content Validity Ratio) and exploratory/confirmatory factor analysis ($EFA$/$CFA$).
- **Instrument Reliability**: Document internal consistency metrics:
  - *Cronbach's Alpha ($\alpha$)* or *McDonald's Omega ($\omega$)* for survey scales (standard threshold $\ge 0.70$ or $0.80$).
  - *Inter-Rater Reliability*: Cohen's Kappa ($\kappa$) or Intraclass Correlation Coefficient ($ICC$) for qualitative coding or human observation.
