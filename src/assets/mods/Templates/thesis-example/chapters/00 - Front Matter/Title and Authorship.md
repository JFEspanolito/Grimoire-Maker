# Title and Authorship

The title page establishes the formal academic identity, institutional affiliation, and degree candidacy of the manuscript.

## Guidelines for this Section

- **Title Formulation**: Formulate a concise, clear, and unambiguous title (typically 12 to 18 words) that encapsulates the primary variables, target population, and scope of investigation. Avoid vague phrasing, informal idioms, or redundant terms like *"A Study on..."* or *"Investigations into..."*.
- **Author Identity**: Provide your full legal name and existing academic credentials.
- **Institutional Hierarchy**: Detail the institution name, academic faculty, department or institute, and academic program.
- **Degree Candidacy**: Specify the formal degree pursued (e.g., *Thesis presented in partial fulfillment of the requirements for the Degree of Master of Science in Software Engineering*).
- **Advisors and Committee**: State the full names, academic degrees, and institutional affiliations of the primary thesis director/supervisor and co-supervisors.
- **Location and Date**: State the city, state/province, country, and year of submission or oral defense.
