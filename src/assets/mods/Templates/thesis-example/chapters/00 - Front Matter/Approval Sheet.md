# Approval Sheet

The approval sheet records the formal evaluation and endorsement of the thesis by the examining jury or defense committee.

## Guidelines for this Section

- **Institutional Validation**: Verifies that the candidate has completed all revisions requested during the public defense and that the work complies with institutional standards of original scholarly research.
- **Committee Signatures**: Provides formal signature blocks, academic titles, committee roles (Chair, Internal Examiner, External Examiner, Advisor), and the date of signature.

```text
The undersigned examination committee hereby certifies that this dissertation has been
reviewed, defended, and approved in partial fulfillment of the requirements for the
degree of [Degree Name].

Examining Committee:

________________________________________
[Prof. Advisor Name, Ph.D.]

________________________________________

Date of Approval: [Month Day, Year]
```
