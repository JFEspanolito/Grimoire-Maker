# Data Presentation and Findings

The results chapter presents the empirical evidence collected during fieldwork or experimentation in an objective, factual manner, without speculative interpretation.

## Guidelines for Presenting Results

- **Structure by Specific Objectives**: Organize findings sequentially in the exact order of the specific objectives established in Chapter 1.
- **Descriptive Profiling**: Begin by reporting demographic distributions, baseline system characteristics, or environmental variables of the sample.
- **Visual Display Principles**:
  - Every table and figure must be numbered sequentially (e.g., *Table 4.1*, *Figure 4.2*) and referenced explicitly within the narrative text before its appearance.
  - Tables must follow formal publication formatting (e.g., APA 7th style with horizontal rules only; avoid vertical gridlines).
  - Include precise units of measurement, sample size ($N$), and standard errors or confidence intervals where appropriate.
- **Objectivity**: Report what the data directly reveals. Reserve explanations of why things occurred and comparisons with prior studies for Chapter 5 (Discussion).
