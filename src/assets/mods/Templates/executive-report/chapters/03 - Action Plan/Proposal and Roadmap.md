# Proposal and Roadmap

1. **Phase 1 (Weeks 1–4)**: Deploy connection pool proxies and tune read-replica routing weights.
2. **Phase 2 (Weeks 5–8)**: Enable automated multi-region DNS health checks and warm standby capacity.
3. **Phase 3 (Weeks 9–12)**: Conduct live game-day failover exercises and finalize operational sign-off.
