# The Keeper

## Before conflict

The keeper asks each visitor to surrender one painful memory. It believes the bargain is merciful.

## Possible resolutions

- **Persuasion:** Show the keeper the villagers' missing names.
- **Repair:** Replace the broken lens while others distract it.
- **Combat:** Break the lantern's supports and survive its final flare.

Use statistics appropriate to your game and group.

## Consequences

A repaired lantern returns every memory. A shattered lantern returns only fragments. An abandoned tower lights again at midnight.

## Reward

Mara offers the party a place in the village archive and a letter containing a lead for the next adventure.
