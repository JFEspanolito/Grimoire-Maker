# Ember Village

Rain collects in the empty grooves of a memorial stone. Nobody remembers whose names belonged there.

## People

| Person | Wants | Knows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mara, archivist | Save her brother's memory | A ledger records visits to the tower |
| Oren, ferryman | Keep travelers away | The tower's back door opens at low tide |
| Elda, healer | End the nightly light | Its keeper once protected the village |

## Clues

- The ledger contains payments for lamp oil long after the keeper died.
- A child's drawing shows a lantern with a cracked blue lens.
- The ferryman remembers a stairway beneath the tower.

Every clue leads toward the watchtower; a missed roll should change the cost, not stop the story.
