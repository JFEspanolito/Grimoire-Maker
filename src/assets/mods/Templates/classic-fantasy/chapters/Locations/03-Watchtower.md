# The Watchtower

## Ground floor

The main door is barred from inside. The low-tide entrance reaches a flooded storeroom.

## Spiral stair

Names have been scratched into every step. Speaking one aloud restores a brief memory but alerts the keeper.

## Lantern room

A cracked blue lens turns above a basin of ash. Covering it stops the light for one night; repairing it releases the memories permanently.

## Discovery

The keeper is trapped by a promise to preserve the village at any cost. The lantern mistakes forgetting for protection.
