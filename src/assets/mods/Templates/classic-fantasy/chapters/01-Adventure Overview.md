# The Lantern of Ash

An old watchtower lights up each midnight. By morning, another villager has forgotten someone they love.

## Adventure at a glance

- **Goal:** Recover the lantern before its keeper erases the village's history.
- **Starting point:** The market square, during a memorial with no names.
- **Pressure:** Each night costs another memory.

## Opening scene

Mara, the village archivist, offers the party a sealed letter addressed in their own handwriting. Inside is a warning: do not trust the light.

## Running the adventure

Use the Village chapter for investigation, the Watchtower for exploration, and the Keeper for the final encounter. Replace the names and stakes to build your own adventure.
